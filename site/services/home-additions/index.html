Capabilities

Our Home Addition Services

Every project's scope is tailored. Here's what we handle in-house — permits, trades, and materials included.

— 01

Room Additions

Ground-level room additions that extend the footprint of your home. Common projects include adding a bedroom, expanding a master suite, adding a home office, or extending a family room. Room additions require foundation work (monolithic slab is standard in Tampa), framing, roofing tie-in to the existing structure, exterior siding or stucco match, and full interior finish including HVAC, electrical, plumbing (if applicable), drywall, flooring, paint, and trim. Room additions cost $80-$200 per square foot in Tampa (per HomeGuide). A 300 sq ft bedroom addition runs approximately $24,000-$60,000.

— 02

Second-Story Additions

Adding a full or partial second floor to a single-story home. Second-story additions require structural engineering to verify the existing foundation and walls can support the additional load — reinforcement is often needed. Cost runs $100-$300 per square foot (per multiple Tampa-market sources), significantly higher than ground-level additions due to structural reinforcement, temporary roofing, and the complexity of building above an occupied home. A 600 sq ft second-story addition (2 bedrooms + bathroom) typically costs $60,000-$180,000. Timeline: 3-6 months.

— 03

Garage Conversions

Converting an attached or detached garage into livable space — bedroom, home office, game room, or in-law suite. Garage conversions cost $15,000-$40,000 in the Tampa market. The scope includes insulating walls and ceiling, installing flooring (the existing garage slab may need leveling or a moisture barrier), adding HVAC ductwork or a mini-split system, framing and drywalling the garage door opening, adding windows for egress, and running electrical circuits. Garage conversions require a building permit in Tampa. Note: converting your garage eliminates covered parking, which may affect resale value and HOA compliance — verify HOA rules before proceeding.

— 04

Sunrooms and Florida Rooms

Enclosed sunrooms and Florida rooms — screen-to-glass conversions, new construction, or adding an enclosed space off the back of the home. Sunrooms range from $20,000-$70,000 depending on size, glass quality, and whether the space is climate-controlled. A basic 3-season sunroom (glass enclosure without full HVAC) costs $20,000-$35,000. A fully conditioned 4-season Florida room with insulated glass, HVAC tie-in, and finished interior runs $40,000-$70,000. Florida rooms are especially popular in Tampa — they provide enclosed, climate-controlled living space usable year-round despite summer heat and rain.

Permitting for Home Additions in Tampa

Every home addition in Tampa requires a building permit — no exceptions. Here is what to expect:

  • General building permit — Required for all additions. Covers structural, framing, and roofing. Cost: $200-$1,500+ depending on project value.
  • Plumbing permit — Required if the addition includes a bathroom, wet bar, or laundry. Separate permit from the general.
  • Electrical permit — Required for all additions (new circuits, outlets, lighting). Separate permit.
  • Mechanical (HVAC) permit — Required if extending existing ductwork or adding a new system.
  • Structural engineer's report — Required for second-story additions and any load-bearing modifications. Cost: $300-$800.
  • Setback and zoning review — The addition must comply with your lot's setback requirements (distance from property lines). Hillsborough County zoning will review this as part of the permit application.

Permit approval typically takes 10-15 business days in Hillsborough County. We handle the entire permitting process — application, plan submission, and scheduling all required inspections.

Our Process

Our Home Addition Process

Every project runs the same disciplined path. No surprises, clear numbers, one point of contact from first visit to final walkthrough.

i.

Free on-site consultation

We visit your home, discuss your goals, assess the existing structure, and review lot constraints (setbacks, easements, HOA rules). This visit is free and takes 60-90 minutes.

ii.

Design and engineering

We produce plans for the addition and coordinate structural engineering if needed. For second-story additions, engineering is always required. You approve the design before we submit for permits.

iii.

Detailed written estimate

Line-by-line pricing for foundation, framing, roofing, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, insulation, drywall, flooring, paint, trim, and contingency. No hidden fees.

iv.

Permitting

We pull all required permits and handle the zoning review. Typical permit timeline: 10-15 business days.

v.

Construction

Foundation, framing, roof tie-in, mechanical rough-in, insulation, drywall, flooring, fixtures, paint, and trim. We tie the addition into the existing home seamlessly — matched roofline, matched exterior finish, matched interior trim profiles.

vi.

Inspections and final walkthrough

Inspections at each required stage. After final inspection and certificate of occupancy, we walk through the completed addition together. You approve before final payment.
