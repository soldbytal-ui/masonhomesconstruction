Countertop Installation in Tampa, FL

Home / Services / Countertop Installation

Mason Homes installs granite, quartz, marble, and butcher block countertops in kitchens and bathrooms throughout Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, and the Tampa Bay area. We manage the full process — template, fabrication, and installation — coordinating directly with Tampa Bay fabricators to keep lead times under 3 weeks from template to final installation.

Countertop replacement is one of the highest-impact kitchen upgrades. We install countertops as standalone projects and as part of full kitchen and bathroom renovations.

Countertop Materials and Pricing

Transparent Tampa market pricing by project scope. Real numbers from verified 2026 data sources — not rough averages.

Granite Countertops

Granite starts at $27/sq ft installed (per AGS Stone, Tampa). Granite is a natural stone — every slab is unique in pattern and color. It requires professional sealing at installation and annual resealing to prevent staining. Granite is heat-resistant (you can set a hot pan on it without damage) and scratch-resistant. Popular granite colors in Tampa kitchens include White Ice, Giallo Ornamental, New Venetian Gold, and Black Galaxy.

Installed cost: Starting at $27/sq ft (per AGS Stone). Mid-range granite runs $35-$60/sq ft installed. Premium exotic slabs reach $80-$100+/sq ft.

Quartz Countertops

Quartz is an engineered stone — ground natural quartz bound with resin. It does not require sealing, resists staining, and handles Tampa's humidity without any maintenance concerns. Quartz is the most popular countertop material in the Tampa market. Brands include Caesarstone, Cambria, Silestone, MSI, and Viatera.

Installed cost: Starting at $50/sq ft (per multiple Tampa fabricators). Mid-range quartz runs $55-$80/sq ft installed. Premium designs with veining that replicates marble reach $80-$120/sq ft.

Quartz vs. granite — full comparison →

Marble Countertops

Marble is a premium natural stone prized for its veining and depth. Carrara, Calacatta, and Statuario are the most requested marble varieties. Marble is heat-resistant but softer than granite — it scratches and etches more easily, particularly from acidic substances (lemon juice, vinegar, wine). Marble requires sealing at installation and resealing every 6-12 months.

Installed cost: $60-$150/sq ft (per multiple sources). Calacatta Gold and Statuario command the highest prices.

Butcher Block Countertops

Solid wood countertops — typically maple, walnut, or white oak. Butcher block adds warmth and texture, especially in farmhouse and transitional kitchen designs. It requires regular oiling (every 1-3 months) and is susceptible to water damage if not maintained. In Tampa's humidity, we recommend butcher block as an accent surface (island top or prep area) rather than a primary countertop material near sinks.

Installed cost: $40-$80/sq ft (per multiple sources).

Process

The Template-Fabrication-Installation Process

Step 1: Template (Day 1)

After your cabinets are installed and level, a templator visits your home with a digital laser measuring system. They map the exact dimensions of every countertop section, mark sink and cooktop cutout locations, and note edge profiles and backsplash heights. Digital templating is accurate to 1/16 of an inch.

Important: Cabinets must be fully installed and secured before templating. If you are doing a full kitchen remodel, countertop templating happens after cabinet installation is complete. See our kitchen remodeling services →

Step 2: Fabrication (5-10 Business Days)

The fabrication shop cuts and polishes your countertops from the slab you selected. This includes the edge profile (eased, beveled, bullnose, ogee, waterfall), sink cutout, cooktop cutout, and any specialty cuts for corners or angles. Fabrication typically takes 5-10 business days after templating.

Step 3: Installation (Day of Install)

The installation crew brings the finished countertop sections to your home and sets them on the cabinets. Sections are joined with color-matched epoxy seams. The undermount sink is installed and connected. Backsplash pieces are set if included. Most kitchen countertop installations take 2-4 hours. Bathroom vanity tops take 1-2 hours.

Total timeline from template to installation: 2-3 weeks for standard projects. Rush fabrication is available from some shops at an additional fee.

Our Process

Our Countertop Installation Process

Every project runs the same disciplined path. No surprises, clear numbers, one point of contact from first visit to final walkthrough.

i.

Free on-site consultation

We visit your home, measure the approximate countertop area, discuss material options, and show you samples. This visit is free and takes about 30-45 minutes.

ii.

Slab selection

For granite and marble, we take you to the fabricator's slab yard so you can see and select the exact slab that will become your countertops. Quartz is consistent slab-to-slab, so slab yard visits are optional.

iii.

Detailed written estimate

Line-by-line pricing for material, fabrication, installation, sink cutout, edge profile, and any demolition/removal of existing countertops. No hidden fees.

iv.

Cabinet readiness check

We verify your cabinets are level, secure, and ready for templating. If cabinets need shimming or reinforcement, we handle that before scheduling the templator.

v.

Template → Fabrication → Installation

The 3-step process described above. We coordinate scheduling with the fabrication shop and keep you informed at each step.

vi.

Final walkthrough

We inspect every seam, check sink alignment, verify edge profiles, and ensure you are satisfied before final payment.
