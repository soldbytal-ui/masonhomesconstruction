Capabilities

Our Whole Home Renovation Services

Every project's scope is tailored. Here's what we manage directly — permits, trades, and materials included.

— 01

Open Floor Plan Conversions

Removing walls between kitchens, living rooms, and dining rooms to create open-concept layouts. Load-bearing wall removal requires a structural engineer's report ($300-$800) and a City of Tampa or Hillsborough County building permit ($200-$800). We handle the engineering, permitting, and construction. Open-concept conversions add $8,000-$25,000 per wall depending on structural requirements (per Estimate Florida Consulting). This is the single most requested change in Tampa homes built before 2000.

— 02

Multi-Room Remodels

Renovating 2 or more rooms as a coordinated project — typically kitchen plus bathrooms, or kitchen plus flooring throughout the home. Multi-room projects save 10-20% compared to doing rooms individually because trades mobilize once, permits are combined, and materials are ordered in bulk. We sequence the work so you maintain access to at least one bathroom and one living area throughout the project.

— 03

Full Gut Renovations

Stripping the home down to studs and rebuilding from the inside out. A full gut includes demolition, structural assessment, framing modifications, new plumbing, new electrical, new HVAC, insulation, drywall, flooring, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, paint, trim, and fixtures. Gut renovations in Tampa often uncover knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing, or termite damage in older homes — we budget a 10-15% contingency for hidden conditions.

— 04

Structural Modifications

Adding or removing walls, widening doorways, reinforcing floors for tile or heavy fixtures, raising ceilings, and modifying roof lines. All structural work requires engineering and permitting. Tampa homes on slab foundations require concrete cutting for plumbing relocation — add $2,000-$5,000 for slab work depending on scope. Tampa building permit guide →

— 05

Design Coordination

We work with your architect or interior designer, or we can connect you with Tampa-based design professionals from our network. Design coordination covers space planning, material selection, fixture specification, lighting layout, and finish schedules. For whole home projects, we recommend completing the design phase fully before breaking ground — this avoids change orders that add 15-25% to project cost.

— 06

Flooring Throughout the Home

Consistent flooring from room to room — luxury vinyl plank (LVP), porcelain tile, hardwood, or engineered hardwood installed throughout the entire home in one phase. LVP is the most popular whole-home flooring choice in Tampa for its humidity resistance, water resistance, and fast installation time. See our flooring services →

Our Process

Our Whole Home Renovation Process

Every project runs the same disciplined path. No surprises, clear numbers, one point of contact from first visit to final walkthrough.

i.

Free on-site consultation

We walk through your entire home, discuss your goals room by room, and identify structural, mechanical, and code considerations. This visit is free and takes 60-90 minutes.

ii.

Scope definition and design

We define the full scope of work, produce floor plans, and coordinate material selections for every room. You approve the complete design package before construction begins.

iii.

Detailed written estimate

Line-by-line pricing for every room, every trade, every material. Permits, labor, materials, contingency, and timeline — all documented. No hidden fees.

iv.

Permitting

We pull all required permits from the City of Tampa or Hillsborough County. A whole home renovation typically requires a general building permit plus separate plumbing, electrical, and mechanical permits. Typical total permit cost: $500-$2,000+ depending on scope (per City of Tampa). Permit approval typically takes 10-15 business days.

v.

Construction phases

We sequence the work logically: demolition, structural, rough plumbing and electrical, HVAC, insulation, drywall, flooring, cabinets, countertops, tile, fixtures, paint, and trim. You receive weekly progress updates and have access to your project manager at all times.

vi.

Inspections and final walkthrough

Inspections at each required stage (framing, rough-in, insulation, final). After final inspection approval, we do a room-by-room walkthrough with a punch list. You approve before final payment.

Living in Your Home During a Whole Home Renovation

For multi-room remodels, most homeowners stay in the home. We sequence work so you always have access to at least one bathroom and a living area. Expect noise, dust, and limited access during active phases.

For full gut renovations, plan to move out. A gut renovation means no functioning kitchen, no functioning bathrooms, and active demolition with dust and debris throughout the home. Typical move-out duration: 4-10 months depending on project scope.

We can phase a gut renovation to keep part of the home livable — this adds 30-50% to the timeline but allows you to stay on-site. Read more about living in your home during a remodel →
