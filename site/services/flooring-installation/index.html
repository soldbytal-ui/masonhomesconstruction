Flooring Installation in Tampa, FL

Home / Services / Flooring Installation

Mason Homes installs tile, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), hardwood, engineered hardwood, laminate, and natural stone flooring throughout Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, and the Tampa Bay area. We handle single-room replacements and whole-home flooring projects, including subfloor preparation, moisture testing, removal of existing flooring, and installation.

Florida's humidity changes the flooring conversation. In Tampa, where average humidity runs 74% year-round, material selection matters more than in drier climates. LVP and porcelain tile are the two most popular choices in the Tampa market — both handle moisture without warping, buckling, or mold growth. Solid hardwood can warp in Florida's climate without strict humidity control (whole-home dehumidification and consistent AC use).

Our Flooring Installation Services

Every project's scope is tailored. Here's what we handle in-house — permits, trades, and materials included.

— 01

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

LVP is the most requested flooring material in Tampa. It is 100% waterproof, handles humidity without expansion or contraction, and installs quickly over most existing subfloors. Modern LVP products from brands like COREtec, Shaw Floorte, and Mohawk RevWood closely replicate the look of hardwood at a lower cost. LVP installation in a standard 1,500 sq ft home takes 2-3 days. Compare LVP and tile →

— 02

Porcelain and Ceramic Tile

Tile is the traditional Florida flooring choice — zero moisture absorption (porcelain absorbs less than 0.5% water), extremely durable, and available in wood-look, stone-look, and decorative patterns. We install floor tile in kitchens, bathrooms, entryways, and whole-home applications. Large-format tiles (24x24 and larger) require a level subfloor — we handle leveling compound and backer board prep as needed. Tile installation takes 3-7 days for a standard home including mortar cure time and grouting.

— 03

Hardwood Flooring

Solid hardwood (3/4-inch oak, hickory, maple) is available for Tampa homes with whole-home dehumidification systems. Without humidity control, solid hardwood in Florida will expand and contract seasonally, causing gaps, cupping, or buckling. We install and finish site-finished and prefinished hardwood. Hardwood installation takes 3-5 days for a standard room, plus 2-3 days for site-applied finish (sand, stain, polyurethane). Read our Florida humidity flooring guide →

— 04

Engineered Hardwood

Engineered hardwood handles Florida humidity better than solid hardwood. Its layered construction (real hardwood veneer over plywood or HDF core) resists expansion and contraction. Engineered hardwood is a strong option for Tampa homeowners who want the look and feel of real wood with better moisture stability. Installation takes 2-4 days for a standard home.

— 05

Laminate Flooring

Laminate is a budget-friendly option that replicates wood and stone looks. Modern laminate with water-resistant cores performs well in most rooms, though we do not recommend laminate in bathrooms or laundry rooms where standing water is common. Laminate installs as a floating floor — no glue or nails — and takes 1-2 days for a standard room.

— 06

Natural Stone

Marble, travertine, and slate tile installation. Natural stone requires sealing upon installation and periodic resealing (every 1-2 years) to prevent staining. Stone flooring is most commonly requested for entryways and bathrooms in Tampa homes. Installation timelines are similar to porcelain tile: 3-7 days depending on scope.

Our Flooring Installation Process

Every project runs the same disciplined path. No surprises, clear numbers, one point of contact from first visit to final walkthrough.

i.

Free on-site consultation

We visit your home, measure all rooms, evaluate the existing subfloor condition, and discuss material options. This visit is free and takes about 45-60 minutes.

ii.

Material selection

We help you choose the right flooring for each room based on usage, humidity exposure, budget, and aesthetics. We bring samples to your home so you can see materials in your actual lighting.

iii.

Detailed written estimate

Line-by-line pricing for materials, subfloor prep, removal of existing flooring, installation labor, transitions, and trim. No hidden fees.

iv.

Subfloor preparation

We remove existing flooring, check for moisture (moisture meter testing on concrete slabs is standard in Tampa), level the subfloor with self-leveling compound where needed, and install backer board for tile applications.

v.

Installation

Our crew installs your flooring, cuts and fits around obstacles, installs transitions between rooms and at doorways, and replaces or installs new baseboards and quarter-round trim.

vi.

Final walkthrough

We walk through every room together. You approve before final payment.

Florida Humidity and Your Flooring

Tampa's subtropical climate creates specific challenges for flooring:

  • Concrete slab moisture: Most Tampa homes are built on concrete slabs. Slabs can wick moisture upward, especially in older homes without vapor barriers. We perform moisture testing before every installation — if moisture levels exceed manufacturer thresholds, we apply a moisture barrier or recommend a flooring type that handles higher moisture.
  • Air conditioning dependency: Homes without consistent AC use (seasonal homes, vacation properties) experience wider humidity swings. LVP and tile are the safest choices for these homes. Solid hardwood should only be installed in homes with year-round climate control.
  • Bathroom and kitchen flooring: We recommend tile or LVP for all wet areas. Hardwood and laminate in bathrooms will eventually fail in Florida's climate, regardless of the product's water-resistance rating.
  • Power outage considerations: Extended power outages mean no AC and no dehumidification. LVP and tile flooring survive multi-day power outages without damage. Hardwood floors may cup or buckle if humidity exceeds 65% for extended periods.
