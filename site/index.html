Common Questions

Planning your project.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Tampa? +

Typical planning ranges vary based on scope, site conditions, materials and structural requirements. In the Tampa market, cosmetic updates typically start around $10,000; mid-range projects (cabinet replacement, quartz counters, appliance package, upgraded lighting) generally fall in the $25,000–$60,000 range; full gut renovations at the higher end can reach $120,000 or more. See our cost guides for a breakdown by scope.

Do I need a permit for home renovation in Tampa? +

Most residential projects require permits from City of Tampa Construction Services or Hillsborough County Building Services. Fees vary based on job valuation. Mason Homes coordinates permitting and inspections as part of the construction process.

How long does a whole home remodel take? +

Three to six months is typical. Kitchens usually run 4–8 weeks; bathrooms 3–5 weeks. County permit review timelines vary by scope and current volume — check City of Tampa or Hillsborough County Building Services for current queue times.

What areas does Mason Homes serve? +

Primarily Hillsborough, Pinellas and Pasco counties — including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Wesley Chapel, Riverview, Carrollwood and surrounding communities. Larger projects are considered in Manatee, Sarasota and other Gulf Coast areas. See our service areas for details.

How do I begin a project with Mason Homes? +

Submit the inquiry form at the top of this page or start a project. We'll follow up to discuss your project scope, location, timing and budget, and arrange an on-site consultation.
