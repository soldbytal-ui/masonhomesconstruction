  • Yes for most projects. Structural changes, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, or window/door replacement all require a permit from the City of Tampa or Hillsborough County. Cosmetic work like painting, tiling, and cabinet refacing does not. Permit fees typically run $250–$1,500 based on job valuation — a kitchen remodel usually falls in the $400–$900 range.
  • A full home renovation in Tampa typically takes 3–6 months depending on scope, permit timelines, and material lead times. A single kitchen remodel runs 4–8 weeks; a bathroom 3–5 weeks. Hillsborough County residential permit review is currently 10–15 business days for standard remodels; ADUs and additions are running 4–8 weeks due to elevated post-hurricane volume.
