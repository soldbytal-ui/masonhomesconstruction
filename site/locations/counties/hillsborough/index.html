Hillsborough County

Mason Homes across Hillsborough.

Hillsborough County is the heart of the Tampa Bay metro and our home county. Mason Homes operates from a studio at 5816 Little River Dr in Tampa and works across every Hillsborough community — from historic South Tampa bungalows to brand-new construction in eastern Plant City. Permitting flows through three different jurisdictions depending on where your home sits: City of Tampa, City of Plant City, or Hillsborough County Development Services for everything else.
