Manatee County

Mason Homes across Manatee.

Manatee County is south of Hillsborough and home to Lakewood Ranch — one of the largest master-planned communities in the United States. Bradenton anchors the county's older urban core, while Lakewood Ranch and the eastern developments represent some of the region's most active premium-remodel and custom-home markets. Mason Homes serves Manatee County by appointment, with project scopes typically large enough to justify the travel distance from Tampa.
