Home / Locations / Lutz

Mason Homes serves Lutz homeowners with full-scope residential construction — kitchens, baths, whole-home renovations, additions, ADUs, and ground-up custom builds.

Lutz sits at Tampa's northern Hillsborough boundary, blending suburban developments with rural acreage and horse-country lots. Cheval and Heritage Harbor are gated golf-course communities with high-end homes; outside those gates, Lutz contains larger lots — half-acre to multi-acre properties — where additions, ADUs, and custom-home rebuilds are common. Housing ranges from 1980s ranch homes to recent custom builds.

Project Mix

What we build in Lutz.

The Lutz project mix skews toward additions and custom homes more than typical suburban Tampa. Detached pool houses, primary-suite additions, and ADU construction are routine here because lot sizes accommodate them. In the gated communities, kitchen and master-bath renovations dominate; outside the gates, scope sizes tend to be larger.

Local Permits & Codes

What's specific to Lutz.

Lutz's larger lot sizes and rural-zoned parcels mean septic-and-well construction is common (no city water/sewer in many areas). Additions that increase bedroom count may require septic upgrades — a budget item we account for during scoping.

We handle every part of the permit process — applications, inspection scheduling, sub-permits across electrical/plumbing/mechanical, and final certificate of occupancy. The homeowner's only role is to be present (or arrange access) for inspections.
