Home Remodeling in Wesley Chapel, FL

Home / Locations / Wesley Chapel

Mason Homes is a licensed general contractor serving Wesley Chapel homeowners in Epperson, Wiregrass Ranch, Meadow Pointe, and Seven Oaks. We handle kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, home additions, custom home building and ADU construction in Pasco County and surrounding communities. Flooring and countertop installation are handled as components of larger project scopes.

Wesley Chapel is one of Tampa Bay's fastest-growing areas. Most homes here were built between 2000 and 2025 — the structures are sound, but the finishes are builder-grade. Laminate countertops, stock cabinets, basic tile, and contractor-grade fixtures are the norm. Upgrading these finishes is the most cost-effective way to increase both livability and resale value without a full renovation.

Neighborhoods We Serve in Wesley Chapel

Epperson (33543)

A master-planned community anchored by a Crystal Lagoon — one of the few in the Tampa Bay area. Epperson homes are 2010s-2020s construction with modern open floor plans. Common projects: replacing builder-grade laminate countertops with quartz, upgrading basic cabinets to semi-custom or custom shaker-style, and adding tile backsplash. Epperson has a community HOA — exterior modifications need approval before permitting.

Wiregrass Ranch (33543, 33544)

A large master-planned community surrounding The Shops at Wiregrass. Homes range from 1,500 sq ft starter homes to 3,500+ sq ft executive homes built between 2005 and 2020. Wiregrass kitchens in older sections (2005-2012) are prime candidates for full remodels — 12-15 years is when builder-grade finishes start showing wear and dating the home. Kitchen remodels in Wesley Chapel run $20,000-$80,000 with custom cabinets and natural stone averaging $40,000-$80,000 (per Salt & Pepper).

Meadow Pointe (33543, 33544)

One of Wesley Chapel's original master-planned communities, with homes dating to the early 2000s. Meadow Pointe homes are among the oldest in the area and most likely to need full kitchen and bathroom renovations. Many still have the original tile flooring, Formica countertops, and builder-basic light fixtures installed 20+ years ago.

Seven Oaks (33544)

A gated community with homes built in the 2000s-2010s. Seven Oaks features larger lots than many Wesley Chapel communities, making it a candidate for home additions and ADU construction. Room additions cost $80-$200 per square foot in the Tampa Bay area (per HomeGuide). ADUs on qualifying lots are increasingly popular for multigenerational living and rental income.

Most Common Wesley Chapel Remodeling Projects

Builder-Grade Kitchen Upgrades

The signature Wesley Chapel project. Most homes here came with 42-inch oak or thermofoil cabinets, laminate countertops, and basic builder hardware. A targeted kitchen upgrade — replacing countertops with quartz ($50+/sq ft installed per multiple sources), swapping cabinet doors or refacing ($5,000-$12,000), adding a tile backsplash ($800-$3,000), and upgrading lighting ($2,000-$5,000) — runs $15,000-$35,000 and avoids a full gut renovation.

For homeowners wanting a complete kitchen overhaul, full Wesley Chapel kitchen remodels with custom cabinets and natural stone countertops run $40,000-$80,000 (per Salt & Pepper).

Permitting in Wesley Chapel

Wesley Chapel is in Pasco County. Permits are issued through Pasco County Building Services — not the City of Tampa or Hillsborough County.

  • Requires a permit: Structural changes, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, window/door replacement, additions, new construction
  • No permit needed: Painting, cabinet refacing, countertop replacement, flooring, hardware
  • Typical permit cost: $200-$800 depending on scope
  • Approval timeline: 10-15 business days for standard residential permits (Pasco County)
  • HOA review: Most Wesley Chapel communities have HOAs — get exterior modification approval before pulling the county permit

We handle all Pasco County permitting and inspections as part of every Wesley Chapel project.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Wesley Chapel? It depends on scope. A targeted upgrade — quartz countertops, cabinet refacing, backsplash, and lighting — runs $15,000-$35,000. A full kitchen remodel with custom cabinets and natural stone countertops averages $40,000-$80,000 (per Salt & Pepper). Builder-grade Wesley Chapel homes get significant visual impact from a $20,000-$35,000 finish upgrade because the starting point is so basic.

Do Wesley Chapel permits go through Pasco County? Yes. Wesley Chapel is unincorporated Pasco County. All building permits are filed through Pasco County Building Services. The process: submit plans, wait 10-15 business days for review, schedule inspections at framing, rough-in, and final phases. Permit costs run $200-$800 depending on project scope. We handle the entire permit process.

When should I remodel a Wesley Chapel home built in 2005-2010? Now is the right window. At 15-20 years old, builder-grade finishes are showing wear — laminate countertops are scratched, cabinet door finishes are peeling, tile grout is stained, and fixtures are dated. More importantly, kitchen and bathroom design trends have shifted significantly since 2005. A $30,000-$60,000 kitchen remodel brings a 15-year-old Wesley Chapel home in line with what buyers expect today.

Can I add an ADU to my Wesley Chapel property? It depends on your lot size, zoning, and HOA restrictions. Pasco County allows accessory dwelling units on qualifying residential lots. Minimum lot size, setback requirements, and utility connections vary by zoning district. HOA deed restrictions may also limit detached structures. We evaluate ADU feasibility during the initial consultation — bring your survey and HOA documents.
