The #1 Request: Open-Concept Kitchen Conversions

Removing the wall between the kitchen and family room is the single most requested kitchen renovation in Brandon. In 1990s Bloomingdale and Valrico homes, the kitchen was designed as a separate room — closed off from the family room by a full or partial wall, often with a small pass-through window. That layout no longer works for how families use their homes.

What an Open-Concept Conversion Involves

  1. Structural assessment — A structural engineer determines if the wall is load-bearing. In most 1990s Brandon homes with truss roof systems, interior walls may or may not be load-bearing — never assume. Engineering report: $300-$800.
  2. Permit — Hillsborough County building permit required for wall removal. Cost: $200-$800. Approval: 10-15 business days.
  3. Wall removal and beam installation — If load-bearing, a steel or LVL beam replaces the wall to carry the roof load. Non-load-bearing walls are simpler and less expensive to remove.
  4. Ceiling, flooring, and finish work — Patching the ceiling where the wall was, extending flooring across the new opening, and finishing drywall transitions.

Open-concept conversion adds $8,000-$25,000 to a kitchen remodel depending on whether the wall is load-bearing and the beam span required (per Estimate Florida Consulting). Detailed open concept guide →

Neighborhood-Specific Kitchen Considerations

Bloomingdale

Bloomingdale homes (1980s-1990s) have the most outdated kitchens in Brandon — original oak or laminate cabinets, Formica countertops, linoleum or dated tile flooring, and fluorescent box lighting. The kitchen footprint is typically 100-150 square feet with a closed-off layout. Most Bloomingdale kitchen remodels include open-concept wall removal, new shaker-style cabinets, quartz countertops, LVP flooring, and recessed lighting.

Electrical panels in 1980s Bloomingdale homes may need upgrading from 100-amp to 200-amp service ($1,500-$3,000) to support modern appliance loads — especially if you are adding a double wall oven, induction cooktop, or high-draw range. Plumbing is generally CPVC or copper and does not require full replacement.

FishHawk Ranch

FishHawk kitchens (2000s-2010s) are structurally fine but visually dated with builder-grade materials. The layout is usually already open or semi-open to the living area, so wall removal is less common. Renovations focus on material upgrades: replacing laminate countertops with quartz ($50/sq ft installed per AGS Stone), swapping stock cabinets for semi-custom or custom, upgrading to stainless appliances, and replacing builder-grade flooring with LVP or tile.

FishHawk renovation costs run 15-25% less than Bloomingdale for comparable finish quality because the infrastructure is modern and layouts require less structural work. FishHawk HOA requires architectural review for any exterior-visible changes.

Valrico

Valrico's housing mix includes older ranch homes (1970s-1980s) and newer subdivisions (2000s). Older Valrico homes may need the same plumbing and electrical updates as Bloomingdale. Newer Valrico homes align with the FishHawk builder-grade upgrade profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Brandon, FL? A cosmetic refresh costs $10,000-$25,000. A mid-range renovation with new cabinets, countertops, and appliances runs $25,000-$60,000. Adding an open-concept wall removal brings the total to $35,000-$75,000. High-end Brandon kitchen remodels reach $60,000-$95,000 (per Estimate Florida Consulting, NovaCoreBuilders).

How much does it cost to remove the kitchen wall in a Brandon home? Open-concept wall removal adds $8,000-$25,000 to a kitchen remodel depending on whether the wall is load-bearing. A structural engineer's report costs $300-$800. The Hillsborough County building permit costs $200-$800. Non-load-bearing wall removal is on the lower end; load-bearing removal with a steel beam is on the higher end.

Do FishHawk Ranch homes need open-concept conversions? Most FishHawk homes (2000s-2010s) were built with open or semi-open floor plans, so wall removal is less common. FishHawk kitchen remodels focus on replacing builder-grade materials — laminate countertops, stock cabinets, and basic flooring — with quartz, semi-custom cabinets, and LVP. These renovations cost 15-25% less than older Bloomingdale homes.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Brandon? A cosmetic refresh takes 1-3 weeks. A mid-range renovation takes 4-8 weeks. A mid-range renovation with wall removal takes 6-10 weeks. Add 10-15 business days for Hillsborough County permit approval and 4-6 weeks for custom cabinet lead time.

Where do I get a building permit for a kitchen remodel in Brandon? Brandon is unincorporated Hillsborough County. Permits are issued by Hillsborough County Building Services (hcfl.gov/businesses/permits-and-records). Plumbing, electrical, structural, and HVAC changes all require permits. Cost: $200-$800. We pull and manage all permits for you.
